# Product requirements — UTERO Legal

Rebuild Indonesian trademark/legal-service website for business owners seeking information and consultation. Preserve recognizable logo, red `#CE181E`, pale `#F0F3F9`, hero character, navigation, section order and service cards. Canonical origin: `https://legal.uteroindonesia.com`. This specification is **planned**, not implemented.

Scope: nine primary routes in `PAGE_INVENTORY.md`, approved article details, on-brand 404, working verified WhatsApp/phone/email actions, responsive shared layout, accessible menus/FAQ, prerendered route HTML, unique title/description/canonical/OG, sitemap and robots policy. Keep useful legacy article URLs or map reviewed 301 redirects. Avoid publishing 71 WordPress pages wholesale: most are template demos.

Homepage order to reproduce after live comparison: contact bar, navigation, hero, two-step registration, search/registration introduction, benefits, four numbered services (Pendaftaran, Perpanjangan, Pengalihan, Perubahan Merek), team, FAQ, news, approved testimonials only, contact CTA, footer. Service pages explain verified offerings; if packages/prices unconfirmed, offer consultation rather than invented pricing. Do not claim official trademark search, automated tracking, payment or legal outcomes without actual evidence.

Acceptance: all approved routes load directly and through links, no dead CTA or horizontal overflow at 375–1920px, keyboard access and visible focus, ≥44px touch targets, reduced-motion handling, meaningful alt text and no demo content. Target Lighthouse 90+ performance and 95+ accessibility/best practices/SEO on mobile and desktop; targets are not measured results. Deployment and production domain require separate verification under `TEST_PLAN.md`.
