# Project instructions

Before building, read `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/REFERENCE_AUDIT.md`, `docs/CONTENT_AUDIT.md`, `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, and `docs/PAGE_INVENTORY.md`. Reference: https://legal.uteroindonesia.com/. Local `artifacts/reference/data/reference-audit.json` and `artifacts/reference/screenshots/` files are snapshots; reverify live content and visual details before release.

Preserve UTERO Legal identity. Separate verified facts from planned recommendations. Never publish demo posts/pages, invented prices, testimonials, credentials, outcomes, legal guarantees, or fake forms. Review rights to assets and copy. Keep approved content typed and separate from presentation. Preserve canonical slugs and approved article URLs; review redirects individually. Build keyboard/touch/reduced-motion support and route-specific prerendered SEO metadata.

Current project is Vite starter. Architecture, plan and test documents specify target state only. Do not mark gates passed without evidence. External Docker networks, DNS, reverse proxy and TLS require inspection and authorization; do not disrupt unrelated infrastructure. See `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` and `docs/SECURITY.md`.
